Frequently asked questions

What is SixStar Replays?
A highlights channel for Grand Theft Auto VI streams on YouTube and TikTok.
What is GTA6 Clips Daily?
The software we built to find moments, prepare clips and publish them to our own accounts. It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.
Who chooses the clips?
The software suggests moments; a person watches each clip and approves or rejects it.
How often do you post?
A few clips per day on each platform, at fixed times, within the platforms' limits.
Which streamers do you feature?
English-language streamers playing GTA VI on Twitch who are on our allow-list. Every clip credits the streamer.
I'm a streamer — how do I get my clips removed?
Email roxywild228@gmail.com. See For streamers.
Do you collect data about viewers?
No profiles, no tracking. The tool reads public chat only while a stream is live to detect reactions and deletes chat logs after a few days. Details in the Privacy Policy.
Are you affiliated with Rockstar Games, Twitch, YouTube or TikTok?
No. We are an independent fan channel.
