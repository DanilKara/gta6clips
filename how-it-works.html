How it works

SixStar Replays is run by two people. We use our own software, GTA6 Clips, to find, prepare and publish highlights. The software is used only by us, only with our own YouTube and TikTok accounts, and only through the official Twitch, YouTube Data and TikTok Content Posting APIs.

1. Which streams we follow

We only follow English-language Twitch streams in the Grand Theft Auto VI category from channels on our allow-list. A channel is on the list if the streamer gave us permission, or if their channel rules do not prohibit clip channels. Any streamer can opt out at any time — see For streamers.

2. Finding a moment

While a stream is live, the tool reads the public chat and measures how fast people write, how many different viewers join in and how strongly they react. When chat suddenly erupts, the tool marks that moment. Moments where chat is busy but nobody reacts (ads, raids, questions) are skipped.

3. Preparing the clip

  • 15–60 seconds around the moment, cut from the public stream;
  • reframed to vertical 9:16 for phones;
  • English subtitles generated from the streamer's speech;
  • clips with copyrighted background music are not posted to TikTok and need an extra manual check for YouTube.

4. Human review

Every clip waits for one of us to watch it and approve or reject it. Nothing is published without that decision. Most detected moments are rejected.

5. Publishing

Approved clips are scheduled within daily limits (a few per day per platform) and published through the official APIs or uploaded by hand. Each post includes the streamer's name and Twitch link, for example:

He did NOT expect that | KaiCenat GTA 6
Streamer: KaiCenat — https://twitch.tv/kaicenat
#GTA6 #Shorts

6. Removal

If a streamer asks us to remove their clips, we take them down from all platforms and stop following the channel, normally the same day. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
