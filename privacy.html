Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Operator: DanilKara. Contact: roxywild228@gmail.com.

GTA6 Clips is an internal tool that prepares short highlight clips from Twitch streams and uploads them to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no public users, no sign-up and no website accounts. This policy explains what data the tool processes, why, and for how long.

1. Data from Twitch

  • Stream information (channel name, stream title, game, viewer count) from the official Twitch API — to know when allowed streamers are live.
  • Public chat messages of those streams (viewer usernames and message text), read anonymously — only to detect moments when viewers react strongly. Chat logs are kept on our computer for at most 3 days and then deleted automatically. For each detected moment, up to 20 chat lines that show the reaction are kept with the clip's record in our internal database, so we can check why the moment was picked.
  • Video of the stream — a rolling buffer of the last ~20 minutes, so a detected moment can be cut. Older video is deleted continuously. Clip files are deleted within 3 days after the clip is published, rejected or removed.

We only process streams of channels on our allow-list. We do not build profiles of viewers and do not combine chat data with any other data.

2. YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload clips to our own YouTube channel and, when a streamer asks, to delete them. By using our channel's content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's processing of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Access requested: youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube.force-ssl (delete our own videos on a removal request).
  • The only Google account connected is our own channel account. The OAuth token is stored only on our computer and is never shared.
  • We do not access, store or use any data of YouTube viewers — no comments, subscribers, analytics or watch data.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. TikTok

  • The tool uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to upload clips to our own TikTok account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • The only TikTok account connected is our own. The access token and the account's open ID returned by TikTok are stored only on our computer and never shared.
  • We do not access data of other TikTok users.
  • Access can be revoked in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.

4. Notifications

The tool sends us status messages (for example “3 clips are waiting for review”) through a private Telegram bot. These messages contain clip identifiers and technical status, not viewer data.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any of the data above with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Data leaves our computer only as the published clips themselves and the text posted with them.

6. This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no forms.

7. Your rights and requests

Streamers can ask us to remove all clips of their channel and to stop clipping it. Anyone can ask what data about them we hold or ask for it to be deleted. Write to roxywild228@gmail.com; we normally act within 24 hours and answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Our content is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes.
