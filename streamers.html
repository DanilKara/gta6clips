For streamers

We make short highlights of GTA VI streams and always credit the streamer with their name and a link to their Twitch channel. If you like that — great. If you don't, we will stop.

Opt out or remove clips

Email roxywild228@gmail.com from an address we can match to your channel (or write to us from your Twitch / social accounts) and tell us your channel name. We will:

  1. remove all published clips of your channel from YouTube and TikTok;
  2. remove your channel from our allow-list, so no new clips are made;
  3. delete stored files of your stream that we still keep.

This normally happens within 24 hours, and the same day for copyright requests.

Give permission

If you want us to clip your GTA VI streams, write to us with your channel name. You can also tell us how you want to be credited or which kind of moments you prefer.

What we never do

  • post full streams or long segments — only short highlights;
  • remove your name or claim your content as ours;
  • monetise your clips against your wishes.
